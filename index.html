Carry-on only rules
1
CPAP in personal item — never checked. Remove at TSA for separate screening.
2
Liquids: 3.4oz each, 1 quart bag, accessible at top for TSA.
3
Wear heaviest shoes and thickest layer on travel day to save bag space.
4
Roll clothes — shapewear, PJs, lounge — saves 30-40% more space than folding.
5
Turkish towel = beach towel + blanket + coverup. One item, three jobs.
6
Compression socks on before boarding. Reduces swelling on long flights.
7
Board early. Overhead bin fills fast. Priority boarding is worth it.
8
Limit to 3 pairs of shoes max: travel, casual, dress.
CPAP travel must-haves
1
CPAP machine + power cord + mask + headgear
2
Distilled water (small bottle) or plan to buy at destination
3
Power strip — hotel outlets are never where you need them
4
Sleep cap + eye mask in same pouch as CPAP for easy hotel setup
5
Pack CPAP last so it comes out first at hotel
Mid-trip reminders
Refill toiletry bottles if staying 5+ nights
Charge all devices every night before bed
CPAP water — refill every 2-3 nights
Laundry mid-trip if 5+ nights (hotel sink or laundry bag)
